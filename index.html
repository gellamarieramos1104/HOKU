Welcome to the crochet community

Everything you need to start hooking, keep learning, and meet other people who love making things with yarn.

Crochet is a craft where you use a single hook to pull loops of yarn through each other to build fabric. It's portable, forgiving, and easy to begin with just a few supplies.

🪝

Crochet hook

Try a 5 mm (H-8) hook to begin. Soft ergonomic handles help tired hands.

🧵

Yarn

Choose a smooth, light-colored worsted weight yarn so stitches are easy to see.

✂️

Scissors

Small, sharp scissors for trimming ends neatly.

🪡

Yarn needle

A blunt, large-eyed needle for weaving in ends and sewing pieces together.

📍

Stitch markers

Mark the start of a round or a tricky spot. A safety pin works too.

Basic stitches and abbreviations

Patterns use short abbreviations. These are the ones you'll see most. Tap a group to filter.

Abbr.StitchWhat it does
chChainThe foundation. Each loop pulled through the one before it.
sl stSlip stitchJoins rounds or moves yarn across without adding height.
scSingle crochetA short, dense stitch. Great for amigurumi and sturdy fabric.
hdcHalf double crochetA middle height with a soft, quick fabric.
dcDouble crochetA taller stitch. Fast to work and drapes nicely.
trTreble crochetExtra tall for lacy, open fabric.
incIncreaseTwo stitches in the same space to add width.
decDecreaseTwo stitches worked together to reduce width.
st(s)Stitch(es)The loops that make up your fabric.
repRepeatDo the sequence again as many times as stated.

Friendly tips

Count your stitches

Count at the end of every row to catch mistakes early.

Relax your grip

Tight stitches make hands sore and fabric stiff.

It's okay to frog

"Frogging" (rip it, rip it) means unraveling mistakes. Every maker does it.

Take breaks

Stretch your wrists and shoulders every half hour.
